HUTJE

AI Content Studio

HUTJE is an AI-powered content creation tool that turns long-form videos into short-form clips.

Users can review their generated clips and send selected content to supported social platforms, including TikTok, for further review and publishing.

TikTok integration

HUTJE can send approved videos to a connected TikTok account as drafts. The user can then review, edit and publish the content from TikTok.
