HUTJE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. About HUTJE

HUTJE is an AI-powered content creation tool that helps users transform long-form videos into short-form content and manage selected content for supported social platforms.

2. Information processed

When a user connects a supported social media account, HUTJE may process information required to provide that integration, such as account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization credentials, and publishing status information.

3. TikTok data

When a user connects TikTok, HUTJE may access basic TikTok account information authorized by the user and use the permissions granted by the user to send selected videos to TikTok as drafts.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and operate HUTJE features, including connecting authorized accounts, sending user-selected content to supported platforms, and tracking the status of those actions.

5. Data sharing

HUTJE does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms when necessary to perform actions explicitly requested by the user.

6. Security

HUTJE is designed to limit exposure of authentication credentials and other sensitive account information. Users should keep their own account credentials secure.

7. User control

Users can disconnect supported social accounts from HUTJE. Disconnecting an account prevents HUTJE from using that connection for future actions.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HUTJE develops or its integrations change.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the HUTJE project owner.
